Autonomous defence networks once created to protect the world's major cities entered a catastrophic system failure.

Entire districts were sealed behind corrupted machine-controlled territory, forcing survivors to retreat behind fractured defensive lines.

The ZERO LINE is now the shifting boundary between reclaimed territory and the sectors humanity has yet to take back.
